# ADR-UADS2-007 — Experience and Policy Learning Governance

Status: PROPOSED / EXPERIMENT REQUIRED  
Date: 2026-09-09

## Direction
Collect operational experience sufficient to recommend model, effort, context, specialist and retry strategies.

## Not yet authorized
Automatic learned-policy activation is not a V2 bootstrap capability.

Before activation:
- establish V1/V2 baseline;
- define evidence thresholds/confidence;
- demonstrate no canonical-governance override;
- version policies;
- provide rollback/kill switch;
- benchmark wins and regressions;
- perform independent review.

No learned policy may change Scope, Architecture, DoD, ADRs or checkpoint authority.
