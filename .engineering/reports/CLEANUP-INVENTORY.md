## Classification meaning

- `VERIFIED_DEAD`: proven safe for a later, independently reviewed removal Work Order. No current candidate meets this bar as a production deletion.
- `PROBABLY_DEAD`: evidence suggests no use but a dynamic mechanism remains unexcluded. No current candidate was promoted to this class because evidence is either stale-doc level or insufficient.
- `DUPLICATE_OR_OBSOLETE`: stale-looking material with a bounded replacement/action. CLN-001 is documentation-only and still needs its own review.
- `GENERATED_OR_VENDORED`: generated, cached, fixture, or tool-owned content. It is not hand-cleaned here.
- `UNKNOWN`: evidence is incomplete or the project contract intentionally retains the material.
